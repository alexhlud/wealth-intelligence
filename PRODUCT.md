# Wealth Intelligence

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Long-term investors who watch progress over years. People who contribute
monthly, rebalance rarely, and care more about the trajectory than today's
price. Not day traders.

## Product Purpose

A personal wealth tracking and long-term financial modeling app. It answers
four questions: how has my financial position changed, where is my money
today, where am I heading, and how would a decision today change that.

## Positioning

The distinguishing feature is genuine history. The app stores immutable
snapshots of exact portfolio composition over time, so a user can look at
what they actually held on a given date rather than a reconstruction.

## Operating Context

Users inspect their financial position and long-term trajectory over years.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Runs at approximately $0/month. It handles real financial data for a small
number of isolated users and must work well on a phone.

## Brand Commitments

Calm, analytical, private, quietly ambitious. Confident about the math,
honest about uncertainty. Projections are always labeled as projections.
Never hype, gamified, or urgent.

Do not look like Robinhood, Bloomberg Terminal, a 2015 bank website, a crypto
dashboard, or a generic AI-generated SaaS landing page. No confetti, streaks,
badges, or green-red flashing. Wealth milestones are acknowledged calmly, not
celebrated loudly.

## Evidence on Hand

The product's immutable portfolio snapshots are the real underlying evidence;
future product work must not fabricate financial history.

## Product Principles

- Answer past, present, future, and decision questions in one private workspace.
- Preserve genuine financial history rather than reconstructing it.
- Serve long-term progress, not short-term trading activity.
- Be precise about math and explicit about uncertainty.
- Keep the experience calm and private.
