# Wealth Intelligence

## What it is

A personal wealth tracking and long-term financial modeling app. It
answers four questions: how has my financial position changed, where is
my money today, where am I heading, and how would a decision today
change that.

The distinguishing feature is genuine history. The app stores immutable
snapshots of exact portfolio composition over time, so a user can look
at what they actually held on a given date rather than a reconstruction.

## Who it's for

Long-term investors who watch progress over years. People who contribute
monthly, rebalance rarely, and care more about the trajectory than
today's price. Not day traders.

## Voice

Calm, analytical, private, quietly ambitious. Confident about the math,
honest about uncertainty. Projections are always labeled as projections.
Never hype, never gamified, never urgent.

## Anti-references

Do not look like: Robinhood, Bloomberg Terminal, a 2015 bank website, a
crypto dashboard, or a generic AI-generated SaaS landing page.

No confetti, no streaks, no badges, no green-red flashing. Wealth
milestones are acknowledged calmly, not celebrated loudly.

## Constraints

Runs at approximately $0/month. Real financial data for a small number
of isolated users. Must work well on a phone.
